완성 프롬프트:

2D side-view game character sprite, advanced space-faring civilization, near-future sci-fi fantasy, practical yet elegant tailored clothing in the same design language as the Nimrod sprite (black practical long coat with straps and gloves), only subtle Mesopotamian motif accents (small eight-pointed star, thin metallic lines), NOT ancient costume, NOT mythological robes, NOT gaudy, soft warm rim light on the light-facing edge, no black outline, no text, no watermark
Create a character DESIGN SHEET ATLAS: four equally high rows, exactly four distinct adult characters, each row contains front full-body, right-facing side full-body, and face close-up, same character across its three views. Large landscape canvas, 2048x2048 if possible. Solid pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere behind figures, completely separate rows and figures with generous gaps, no labels. Top-left soft warm edge illumination, refined painterly detail matching Nimrod. Row 1: Lugalbanda, dignified broad-shouldered middle-aged male commander, short dark hair with silver streaks, short groomed beard, tailored slate-gray practical long coat, high collar, lightweight shoulder armor, thin silver lines, ivory inner layers, gloves, practical trousers and boots, NO crown. Row 2: Ninsun, serene elegant middle-aged adult woman, updo, extremely tiny pale-gold horn-shaped hair pin, sage teal and cream tailored long coat-dress, warm resolute face, practical fitted trousers under coat, minimal jewelry. Row 3: Shamhat, noble captivating beautiful adult woman with glossy long wavy hair swept to one side, beauty mark below one eye, gently alluring smile and gaze, refined tailored ivory dress with rose-gold narrow accents, long sleeves and modest slit, modern couture, absolutely NO religious objects or veil, tasteful fully clothed. Row 4: Papsukkal, unmistakably a VERY BEAUTIFUL ADULT WOMAN, slender tall elegant female guard, defined refined face, neat short haircut or tied hair, calm firm eyes, charcoal-silver tailored practical long coat uniform, pale ice-blue accents, lightweight shoulder protection, waiting with hands behind back. Clothing should be brighter than navy/gold/burgundy backgrounds, NO thick black ink outline. Negative visual elements to exclude: ancient costume, toga, loincloth, tiered fringe skirt, royal crown, mythological robe, veil, altar, temple priestess outfit, excessive jewelry, gaudy, black outline.
