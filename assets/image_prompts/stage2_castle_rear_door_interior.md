2D side-view game asset, dark fantasy, ancient Mesopotamian Uruk atmosphere, hand-painted look, clean readable silhouette, consistent with the Nimrod character sprite sheets, no text, no watermark
Create an animation atlas with THREE equal horizontal rows, each row SIX equal rectangular cells, each cell one independent tall door sprite. Solid flat #FF00FF magenta everywhere outside stone/door artwork. NO grid lines, borders, labels, text, people or floor. Identical consistent arch frame stays fixed in every cell, top-left lighting. All 6 frames progressive wooden/iron double doors opening inward: frame1 completely closed, frame2 slightly parted, frame3 quarter open, frame4 half open, frame5 almost open, frame6 COMPLETELY OPEN with pitch-black empty passage visible and leaves tucked into sides. Side-facing scene but doors viewed straight-on, orthographic 2D. Row1 ancient Uruk rear castle gate EXTERIOR: weathered gray stone arch and iron-bronze door leaves. Row2 royal interior floor door: tall rectangular carved wooden frame, gold and bronze fittings, dark mahogany leaves. Row3 same castle rear door from INTERIOR: masonry arch with indoor bronze sconces, iron leaves. Give each full arch padding and separate adjacent cells by magenta. Keep dimensions and lighting identical throughout each animation, only hinge angle changes. 2048x2048 canvas.
